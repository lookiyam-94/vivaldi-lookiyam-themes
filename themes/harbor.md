Harbor (Lookiyam) is a calm night-harbor theme. Deep navy toolbars sit above a darker navy tab bar, with warm off-white text and a pale-gold highlight for focus and selection, like one harbor light over still water. The Start Page shows the Harbor mark: two open breakwaters with a single gold light between them.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Harbor theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds, from the foggy pierhead to the night quay, and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
