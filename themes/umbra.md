Umbra (Lookiyam) is a deep, eclipse-inspired dark theme. Blue-black toolbars and tab bar carry cool silver text, and a pale ice-blue highlight marks focus and selection. The Start Page shows an eclipse: a dark disc edged by a thin crescent of light.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Umbra theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds, from totality to the diamond ring, and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
