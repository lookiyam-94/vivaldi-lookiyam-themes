Cursor Dark (Lookiyam) is a clean, editor-style dark theme for people who live in their code editor. Toolbars sit on a soft #191919 grey, the tab bar one step darker at #141414, with soft white text that is easy on the eyes. A clear link-blue highlight marks focus and selection, and a single white cube sits alone on the Start Page.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Cursor Dark theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All 14 backgrounds and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
