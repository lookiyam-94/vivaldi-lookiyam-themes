Cursor Dark brings the look of the Cursor code editor to Vivaldi, inspired by Cursor, Grok and Grok Bot. The greys come straight from Cursor's built-in dark theme: toolbars in the editor's #191919, the tab bar in the title bar's #141414, with soft white text. Cursor's link blue marks focus and selection, and the white cube sits alone on the Start Page.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Cursor Dark theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All 14 backgrounds and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
