Carbon (Lookiyam) is a dark, minimal theme built around one hot point of light. Steel-grey toolbars sit above an even darker tab bar, with cool off-white text that is easy on the eyes. A single vivid orange highlight marks focus and selection, and the Start Page shows one steel hexagon with a glowing corner, like a carbon atom.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Carbon theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
