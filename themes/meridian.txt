Meridian (Lookiyam) is a crisp, geometric dark theme. Slate-black toolbars and tab bar frame cool silver text, and a bright sky-blue highlight marks focus and selection. The Start Page shows a thin triangle with a single point of light at its apex.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Meridian theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
