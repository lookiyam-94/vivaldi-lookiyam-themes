Bramble (Lookiyam) is a dark hedgerow theme. Plum-black toolbars and a darker tab bar carry soft grey-white text, and a berry-pink highlight marks focus and selection. The Start Page shows the Bramble mark: an arching, thorned cane with a single glowing blackberry hanging from its tip.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Bramble theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds, from the berry moon to the lone thorned stem, and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
