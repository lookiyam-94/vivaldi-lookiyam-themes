Northline is a cold, quiet dark theme with a hint of the northern lights. Blue-black toolbars and tab bar carry icy silver text, and a mint-green highlight marks focus and selection. The Start Page shows a rounded square split by a single vertical line of light.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Northline theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All backgrounds, including an aurora and a compass, and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
