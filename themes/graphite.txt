Graphite is a calm, dark theme in soft graphite greys. The toolbars sit above a deeper tab bar, with near-white text and a gentle blue highlight for focus and selection. The Start Page shows the Graphite mark: an open ring with a bar of blue light passing through its gap.

Part of the Lookiyam themes, made to match the Graphite theme for the Omarchy Linux desktop. All 11 backgrounds, including moody night landscapes, and the build script are at https://github.com/lookiyam-94/vivaldi-lookiyam-themes
